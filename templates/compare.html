What the columns meanShowed up to vote is the share of roll calls where the member voted. Bills introduced counts bills and resolutions the member wrote this Congress; the next two columns count how many of those passed the House and became law. Voted with party counts only votes where most Republicans and most Democrats took opposite sides.
Reading these fairlyNo single column says whether someone is doing a good job. Members in the minority party pass fewer bills. Committee leaders introduce more.
