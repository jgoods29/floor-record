Committees

Most of the {{ ch.name }}'s work happens in committees. Each one handles a subject, like farming, taxes or the military, and reviews bills before the full {{ ch.name }} votes on them. Most bills never make it out.{% if c %} Senate committees also question the President's picks for judges and top jobs before the full Senate votes on them.{% endif %}

Each committee has a chair from the majority party, who decides which bills get a hearing, and a ranking member, the top member from the minority party. Most split their work further into subcommittees.
