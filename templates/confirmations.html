Confirmations

The President picks people for the government's top jobs: judges, cabinet secretaries, ambassadors. Most of them cannot start until the Senate votes yes. This is every one of those votes.

A judge confirmed here can serve for life, so these votes can matter for decades. Only the Senate votes on nominees; the House has no say.
