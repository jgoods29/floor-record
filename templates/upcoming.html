What the labels mean

Full debateNeeds a simple majority to pass: more than half of the members voting. These are often the bigger, more argued-over bills, and members can usually offer changes.
Fast trackNeeds two-thirds to pass and gets a short debate with no changes allowed. Usually used for bills most members already agree on.
