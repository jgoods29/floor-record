Why

Why this was built.

The people in Congress work for us. You cannot hold someone accountable if you do not know what they did.

In November 2026 the House voted on whether to impeach the Secretary of Defense. Whatever you think of that vote, it raised a simple question: how did each member vote, and how do they usually vote?

There was no easy answer. The official record is public, but it is written for people who already know how Congress works. If you do not, it can feel like it was never meant for you.

That is a problem. A representative's whole job is to represent the interests of the people back home. Their votes are the clearest record of whether they are doing it. That record should be as easy to look up as a box score.

So this site was built. Every vote, in plain English, straight from the official record. No spin, no side, no telling you what to think.

What this site stands for

Knowing comes firstThe only way to hold the people representing us accountable is to know what they do.
It should be for everyoneYou should not need to follow politics, or know the jargon, to check on your own representative.
Then you actKnowing is step one. Calling, writing and voting are what change things. This site tries to make that next step one tap away.

The promise

This is a free service, for the people. No tracking, no profit, just knowledge. It does not take sides, and every number links back to the official record so you can check it yourself.
